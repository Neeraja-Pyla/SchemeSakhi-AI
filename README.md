# SchemeSakhi AI 🇮🇳

### Understand government schemes. Know your next step. In your language.

**SchemeSakhi AI** is a Telugu-first AI-powered civic assistant that helps citizens understand government welfare schemes in simple language and explore whether they may meet the eligibility requirements.

## 🎯 The Problem

Many citizens struggle to access government welfare schemes because:
- Scheme guidelines are difficult to understand.
- Information is spread across multiple government websites.
- Eligibility conditions can be confusing.
- People may not know which documents they need or where to apply.

Language and information barriers can prevent people from accessing support intended for them.

## 💡 Our Solution

SchemeSakhi AI makes government scheme information easier to understand through a simple, conversational experience in Telugu and English.

A citizen can:
1. Ask questions about government schemes.
2. Understand scheme benefits and requirements in simple language.
3. Check preliminary eligibility for PM-KISAN.
4. Understand the reasons behind the eligibility result.
5. Explore documents and official application links.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Multilingual AI Assistant:** Ask questions in Telugu or English.
- **Scheme Discovery:** Explore information about four government schemes.
- **Preliminary Eligibility Checker:** Interactive PM-KISAN eligibility assessment.
- **Transparent Results:** Understand eligibility matches and mismatches.
- **Action Guidance:** Find relevant document information and official portals.
- **Responsible AI:** Clear limitations and reminders to verify information with official sources.

## 🏗️ How It Works

1. The citizen asks a question or selects a scheme.
2. The backend processes the request.
3. Gemini helps explain scheme information in accessible language.
4. The PM-KISAN eligibility endpoint evaluates the submitted answers.
5. The frontend displays the result and relevant guidance.

## 🔒 Responsible AI & Limitations

SchemeSakhi AI is an independent project, not an official government service.

Eligibility results are preliminary guidance and do not guarantee government approval. Scheme rules may change, so citizens should verify requirements through the linked official portals.
